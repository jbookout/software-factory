# Acceptance Checks

Read the generated artifacts, not a maker's completion message.

1. Check identity: current client, date, geography, approved shortlist, no previous client names, example data, credentials, project IDs, or obsolete options.
2. Check source fidelity: each claim matches its raw source, year, geography and unit; photos depict the stated property/project and have permitted reuse. Unknown values display as unknown, never zero.
3. Check model inputs against the brief; recompute one complete loan/rent/equity scenario independently. Test zero interest, nonzero down payment and post-maturity hold if allowed. Change every TI selection and read all affected totals.
4. Open Home, Leases, Purchases, Ownership, Demographics, Sources and a property deep link in a browser. Verify current title stays visible while scrolling.
5. Hover and focus map numbers and table addresses. Read the preview's identity and confirm the number remains visible. Click a tile/table/map entry, close its dialog with Escape, and confirm focus returns.
6. At desktop and phone widths, inspect tables, cards and dialogs for overlap, clipping and horizontal overflow. Test keyboard navigation, touch targets and reduced-motion behavior. Check image load, graph labels and chart/table agreement.
7. Read the ZIP's member list and open a freshly extracted copy. Test relative PDF/image links and offline behavior. A multi-file site must be sent as a ZIP or hosted; emailing only index.html loses its assets.
8. For requested PDF, render and inspect every page separately for clipped content and broken pagination. Do not claim the HTML and PDF are synchronized without comparing them.
9. For authorized hosting, probe the final URL and alternate origins. Test root behavior, deep links, assets and password rejection/acceptance. Client-side password prompts, obscure paths, and noindex are not access controls. Protect PDFs and alternate origins too; never place credentials in source or a public bundle.

For revisions, compare unchanged sections against the saved approved baseline. Record checks and unresolved limitations in the environment's evidence/record system. Do not publish internal review notes with the client site.
